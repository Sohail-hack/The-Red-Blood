📖 HOW TO PLAY
🎯 OBJECTIVE

Survive each round by striking your opponent with elemental wind blades to reduce their HP to 0. Win the required rounds to claim match victory!

🎮 CONTROLS
PLAYER 1 (Crimson)
• Move: A D
• Jump: W
• Blue Wind: E
PLAYER 2 / AI (Azure)
• Move: ← →
• Jump: ↑
• Red Wind: 0
⚡ ARENA TIPS

• Pause Anytime: Press ESC during the fight to pause.
• Controlled Attacks: Wind blades have a tactical delay cooldown—time your slashes carefully!
• Platforms: Jump up through stone platforms from underneath.
• Medkits: Periodically drop on platforms to heal +3 HP!
